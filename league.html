06

League stage

Franchises are drawn into groups and play each other once. Group results and point differential decide who advances and how the knockouts are seeded.

07

Knockout stage

The top teams from each group meet in single-elimination quarterfinals and semifinals. Lose and you are out.

08

Championship structure

The two semifinal winners play one championship game for the Season 1 title, with the awards ceremony straight after.

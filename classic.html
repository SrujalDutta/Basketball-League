FAQ

Can't find what you need? Email info@example.com

5v5 teams can register up to 12 players. 3v3 teams can register up to 4 players. Roster changes are allowed until check-in on Friday.

Players must be under the division age as of September 1 of the current season. Proof of age may be requested at check-in.

Full refunds are available until February 15. After that, entry fees can be transferred to next season's tournament.

Day passes are $10 for adults and free for kids under 12. Weekend passes are $20 and available at the door or online.

Youth and high school divisions follow standard high school rules. The Open division follows FIBA rules. Full rulebook is sent to coaches after registration.

Spots are limited

01

Why IBPL?

Get in at the start. Season 1 is the league's launch, and its founding franchises shape what it becomes.

  • A franchise in a structured, officiated national youth league.
  • Your own squad, built at the player auction.
  • Exclusive rights to represent your district.
  • Brand presence across matches, media and community events.
02

Franchise ownership model

Each owner holds one of sixteen franchises for Season 1, builds a squad at the player auction and represents a district.

Shared privatelyFranchise fees and the financial model are shared after your enquiry, not published on the website.
03

Exclusive district rights

Every franchise represents its own district exclusively: no other IBPL franchise in the same territory. Districts are confirmed when the franchise agreement is signed.

09

Frequently asked questions

How much does a franchise cost?

Pricing is shared privately with serious enquiries. Send the form above and the league team will get in touch.

How are players chosen?

Through the player auction. Every registered player enters the pool, and franchises bid to build their squads.

Can a school or academy own a franchise?

Yes. Schools, academies, companies and investor groups can all enquire.

What does exclusive district rights mean?

Your franchise is the only IBPL team representing your district.

About the Indian Basketball Pro League

IBPL is an under-16 franchise league built to give India's young players a professional stage, from the player auction to championship night.

Our story

IBPL began with a simple question: why should young players wait until adulthood to experience professional basketball? Season 1 brings the franchise model, the auction and the big-game atmosphere to under-16 boys.

VisionA professional stage for every young talent

A league where India's best young players are seen, developed and celebrated.

MissionBuild the pathway

Run a fair, well-officiated league that connects grassroots talent to professional standards.

League philosophy

  • Every game counts: every franchise is guaranteed meaningful games.
  • Fair play: certified officials and clear rules.
  • Real teams: squads are built at the player auction.
  • Development first: coaching and clinics alongside competition.

Long-term objectives

  • Grow from Season 1 into an annual national youth league.
  • Expand age groups and add a girls' competition.
  • Build school and district pathways into the league.

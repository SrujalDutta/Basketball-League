Questions

Who can play?

Under-16 boys: players who are under 16 on September 1, 2026, which means born on or after September 2, 2010. Proof of age is checked at Friday check-in.

Do I need a team to register?

No. Teams are formed at the player auction. Register on your own and you'll enter the auction pool.

How does the auction work?

Every registered player enters the auction pool. Teams bid for players, and each player joins the team that wins them. The auction date will be announced and emailed to registered players.

How many players are on a roster?

Up to 12 players per team, and at least 5 to start a game.

Is there a refund policy?

Full refunds until February 15. After that, entry can be moved to next season.

Do spectators need tickets?

Day passes are $10, weekend passes $20, and kids under 12 get in free. Coaches and parents don't register.

What rules do you play by?

Standard high school rules: four 10-minute quarters and a 24-second shot clock. The full rulebook is emailed after registration.
